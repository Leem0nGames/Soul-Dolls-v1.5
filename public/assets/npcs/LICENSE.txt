NPC billboard sprites
=====================
The PNG poses in this folder are from Kenney - Toon Characters:
https://kenney.nl/assets/toon-characters

Source mirror (pinned commit):
https://github.com/shorepine/kenney/tree/3694c6879e487c108f55677be7dd2ca75b07cc3b/2d/Toon%20Characters

Original poses:
- tyler.png: Male adventurer, character_maleAdventurer_idle.png
- vesper.png: Female adventurer, character_femaleAdventurer_idle.png
- merchant.png: Male person, character_malePerson_idle.png
- iris.png: Male person, character_malePerson_talk.png
- mother.png: Female person, character_femalePerson_idle.png
- npc-atlas.png: generated from the five poses above by npm run bake:npc-sprites
